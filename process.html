The Five Phases in Detail

Every engagement follows the same rigorous sequence — because skipping steps is how projects fail.

01
DISCOVER

Discovery — What does success look like?

Before we touch anything, we align on your goals, pull your baseline data, and document exactly how your digital presence is performing today. You can't prove improvement without a starting point.

What you get: Signed agreement with defined KPIs. Baseline performance report. Full access setup.

Kickoff Call Baseline Data Pull Goal Alignment Signed SOW
02
STRATEGY

Strategy — Where is the gap and why?

We audit your site, research your keywords, assess your Google Business Profile, and map your competitive landscape. Then we identify the root causes — not just the surface symptoms — of why you're underperforming.

What you get: Strategy brief with root causes identified, solutions proposed, and your approval before we build anything.

Site Audit Keyword Research Competitive Analysis Root Cause Mapping
03
BUILD

Build — What changes will close the gap?

We build the solution: custom-coded website, on-page SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, and Analytics configuration. Every decision traces back to a specific root cause from the Strategy phase.

What you get: Staging site review. Zero known defects before you give launch approval.

Custom Website Build On-Page SEO GBP Optimization QA Checklist
04
LAUNCH

Launch — Did it perform as expected?

Final QA, deployment, DNS setup, and a 30-day monitoring window. We reset the baseline on launch day so every future report measures from day one of your new site — not some ambiguous "before."

What you get: Live site. Launch baseline report sent to you the day it goes live.

Final QA Deployment DNS Setup Launch Baseline Report
05
OPTIMIZE

Optimize — Are the gains holding?

For subscription clients: you get a written update every Friday and a full performance dashboard every month. We actively monitor, iterate on SEO, and flag anything that needs attention — before it becomes a problem.

What you get: Friday updates. Monthly performance report. Ongoing SEO iteration.

Weekly Friday Updates Monthly Reports SEO Iteration Performance Monitoring
