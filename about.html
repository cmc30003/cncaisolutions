Our Story

We Built This for the Business Owners That Big Agencies Ignore

Local contractors and small businesses deserve the same quality of digital work as any large company. We're here to make that happen.

Why We Started C&C AI Solutions

Most local service businesses — the plumbers, electricians, landscapers, roofers — have a website that cost real money and produces nothing. They were sold a template, handed a login, and left alone.

We believe that's wrong. Local businesses are the backbone of communities. They deserve a digital partner who treats their investment seriously, explains what they're doing in plain language, and proves results in writing.

C&C AI Solutions was founded in Georgia in 2026 to be exactly that kind of partner — diagnosis-driven, results-accountable, and built on a commitment to serve clients before selling to them.

What We Believe

  • Serve before you sell — diagnosis before prescription
  • Prove results in numbers, not words
  • Plain language is a sign of respect
  • Local businesses deserve serious digital work
  • Accountability means owning mistakes and fixing them
  • The best measure of success is a client who understands their own business better

Our Methodology

We don't wing it. Every engagement follows a documented, data-driven process.

🎯

Six Sigma DMAIC

A structured quality framework that ensures every phase of every engagement has a defined purpose, measurable output, and documented result. We fix root causes — not symptoms.

🏢

Burke-Litwin Diagnostic

An organizational analysis model we use during discovery to understand why a client's digital presence is underperforming — not just that it is. The right diagnosis changes everything.

🔄

Action Learning

After every project, we run a retrospective and update our process. Every engagement makes us sharper for the next one. Our methodology improves with every client we serve.

🤝

Servant Leadership

Before any framework: we lead with listening, empower rather than impress, and take full accountability when we fall short. This is our foundation — everything else flows from it.
